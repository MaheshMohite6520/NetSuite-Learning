# Week 19 – Intercompany Elimination Architecture

## Overview

This week focuses on understanding the complete **Intercompany Elimination Architecture** in NetSuite and how organizations eliminate intra-group transactions, ensure accurate consolidated financial statements, manage foreign currency translation differences, strengthen financial governance, and support an efficient period-end close process.

Intercompany Elimination is not simply about removing transactions between subsidiaries.

It is a tightly integrated financial consolidation architecture connecting:

- Intercompany Transactions
- Automated Intercompany Management (AIM)
- Advanced Intercompany Journal Entries (AICJE)
- Intercompany Sales & Purchases
- Inventory Transfers
- Drop Shipments
- Elimination Subsidiaries
- CTA-E (Cumulative Translation Adjustment – Elimination)
- Currency Revaluation
- Consolidated Exchange Rates
- Elimination Journal Entries
- Intercompany Reconciliation
- Financial Consolidation
- Audit Trail Management
- Period-End Close Controls

Every intercompany transaction impacts consolidated financial reporting.

Whether the transaction originates from:

- Intercompany Sales Invoices
- Intercompany Vendor Bills
- Advanced Intercompany Journal Entries (AICJE)
- Intercompany Inventory Transfers
- Intercompany Drop Shipments
- Intercompany Loans
- Intercompany Expense Allocations
- Intercompany Management Fees
- Multi-Currency Intercompany Transactions
- Multi-Subsidiary Business Operations

the organization must eventually answer one critical question:

> Does this transaction represent genuine business with an external party, or is it simply movement of value within the same corporate group?

Because recording intercompany transactions supports operational accounting, but elimination ensures accurate consolidated financial reporting.

## Why This Matters

Understanding Intercompany Elimination Architecture ensures:

- Consolidated financial statements remain accurate
- Internal revenues and expenses are eliminated correctly
- Foreign currency translation differences are handled properly
- Financial close becomes more efficient
- Audit readiness improves
- Financial governance remains effective
- Multi-subsidiary reporting becomes reliable
- Compliance requirements are supported

A misunderstanding of Intercompany Elimination architecture can lead to:

- Overstated consolidated revenue
- Incorrect receivable and payable balances
- Foreign currency translation discrepancies
- Consolidation reporting errors
- Period close delays
- Audit concerns
- Weak financial controls
- Reduced confidence in consolidated financial statements
